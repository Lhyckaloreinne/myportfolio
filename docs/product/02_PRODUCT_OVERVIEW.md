# Product Overview

## Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of the product, including the problem it solves, the intended users, its key features, and the overall user experience. It serves as a shared understanding of the product before detailed requirements and technical implementation begin.

---

# 1. Product Summary

The Personal Career Platform is a modern web application designed to showcase my professional skills, projects, and experiences through recruiter-focused journeys.

Instead of presenting all content on a single portfolio page, the platform organizes information into dedicated specialization areas that allow recruiters to explore the work most relevant to their hiring needs while maintaining one cohesive personal brand.

The platform combines thoughtful design, structured storytelling, and software engineering practices to create an engaging and professional experience.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Traditional portfolios often present every project and skill in a single experience regardless of the visitor's needs.

This creates several challenges:

- Recruiters spend unnecessary time searching for relevant information.
- Different career paths often require different project highlights.
- Maintaining multiple portfolio websites creates duplicated work and inconsistent branding.
- Many portfolios emphasize visual presentation but fail to communicate the thinking and process behind the work.

The challenge is to create a single platform that remains focused for different audiences without sacrificing consistency or maintainability.

---

# 3. Solution

The platform solves these challenges by providing recruiter-focused experiences within one unified website.

Visitors begin with a general introduction before choosing the specialization most relevant to them.

Each specialization presents curated projects, skills, and experiences while sharing the same design language, navigation, and overall user experience.

This approach provides flexibility without requiring multiple independent portfolio websites.

# 5. User Personas

### Persona 1 — Recruiter

Goal:
Quickly evaluate whether the candidate matches the job requirements.

Needs:

- Fast navigation
- Relevant projects
- Resume download
- Contact information

---

### Persona 2 — Hiring Manager

Goal:
Understand both technical ability and professional thinking.

Needs:

- Project process
- Case studies
- Technical skills
- Evidence of organization

---

### Persona 3 — Potential Client

Goal:
Evaluate the quality of work before initiating collaboration.

Needs:

- Portfolio
- Services
- Contact information
- Professional presentation

---

# 6. Value Proposition

The platform provides:

- One cohesive personal brand.
- Multiple recruiter-focused experiences.
- A balance between technical, creative, and organizational skills.
- Well-documented project processes rather than only final outputs.
- A continuously evolving portfolio that grows alongside my career.

# 8. User Journey

The intended user journey is:

Landing Page

↓

Learn who I am

↓

Understand my strengths

↓

Choose a specialization

↓

Explore projects

↓

Review skills and experience

↓

Download resume or make contact

The experience should require minimal effort for visitors to find information relevant to their goals.

---

# 10. Constraints & Assumptions

Current Constraints

- Single developer project
- Portfolio content will continue evolving
- Some specialization areas currently contain fewer projects than others

Assumptions

- Visitors primarily access the platform to evaluate professional qualifications.
- Most users spend only a few minutes browsing.
- Clear organization improves recruiter experience.

---

# 11. Success Metrics

The product succeeds when:

- Visitors can understand the platform within one minute.
- Navigation feels intuitive.
- Recruiters can easily locate relevant projects.
- The platform remains maintainable as additional projects are added.
- The user experience feels cohesive across all specialization pages.

---

# 12. Future Vision

The platform is designed to grow beyond a traditional portfolio.

Future iterations may introduce additional specialization areas, richer project case studies, content management capabilities, and interactive features while preserving the same design philosophy and user experience.
