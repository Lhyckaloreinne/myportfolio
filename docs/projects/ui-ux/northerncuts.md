## 01. Project Overview

### Short Description

Northern Cuts is an e-commerce website designed for a furniture business specializing in handcrafted wooden furniture and customized furniture pieces. The website provides customers with a centralized online space where they can explore the business's furniture offerings, view available products, inquire about custom designs, and place orders without needing to visit the physical location.

### Project Context

The project was developed for a client in the furniture industry. Northern Cuts specializes in wooden furniture and also offers customized tables, chairs, and other furniture pieces based on individual customer requirements.

The website was created to establish an online presence for the business and make its products and services more accessible to customers.

## 02. The Problem

### Background

Northern Cuts primarily operates as a physical furniture business where customers may need to visit the location to see the available furniture and learn more about the products and services.

Because the business specializes in wooden furniture and also accepts customized designs, customers may need to communicate directly with the business to discuss their requirements.

### Problem Statement

The business needed a centralized online platform where customers could discover its furniture offerings, view its work, inquire about customized designs, and place orders without having to physically visit the store for every interaction.

### Existing Challenges

* Customers had limited access to the business's furniture offerings without visiting the physical location.
* Product information and available furniture needed a centralized online space.
* Customers interested in customized furniture needed a convenient way to communicate their requirements.
* The business needed a more accessible way to showcase its work to potential customers.
* Customer inquiries and orders could be brought together through a single online platform.

## 08. Key Design Decisions

### Neutral Visual Palette

**Decision:**
Use neutral colors throughout the website.

**Reason:**
Northern Cuts primarily sells wooden furniture, which naturally contains warm brown and earthy tones. A neutral interface allows the furniture's natural colors and textures to remain visually prominent.

**Impact:**
The visual design complements the products and creates a cohesive relationship between the website interface and the furniture being presented.

---

### Product-Focused Presentation

**Decision:**
Give furniture imagery a strong role in the interface.

**Reason:**
Customers need to visually evaluate furniture before deciding whether to inquire or purchase.

**Impact:**
The website allows customers to discover and visually explore Northern Cuts' work without immediately needing to visit the physical location.

---

### Centralized Online Experience

**Decision:**
Bring product browsing, inquiries, and ordering into one website.

**Reason:**
The business needed a centralized digital space where customers could discover products and communicate with the business.

**Impact:**
Customers can move from discovering furniture to asking questions or placing orders within the same online experience.
