# Design Decision Log

## Purpose

This document captures decisions that affect the visual direction, brand identity, and design language of the Personal Career Platform.

---

## Decision: Shift portfolio visual direction to elegant, warm, neutral, image-led presentation

- Date: 2026-07-29
- Author: Lhycka Loreinne Sulit
- Summary: The portfolio visual direction intentionally changed from a previous editorial/newspaper-like approach toward an elegant, warm, neutral, image-led personal portfolio.
- Rationale:
  - Prioritize visual work, personal identity, and strong imagery.
  - Reduce excessive text, heavy editorial layouts, and card-heavy presentation.
  - Emphasize refined proportions, generous whitespace, and understated interactions.
  - Maintain the existing technology stack and information architecture while updating the visual design guidance.

---

## Notes

This decision applies to all future design and UI updates, including component-level styling, layout composition, and motion behavior.

Future work should follow the updated brand and design system guidance rather than the previous publication-style direction.
