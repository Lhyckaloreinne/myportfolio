# Information Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the information architecture of the Personal Career Platform. It outlines how information is organized, how users navigate the platform, and how content is structured to create a clear, intuitive, and recruiter-focused experience.

The platform is designed to support multiple professional specializations while maintaining a single cohesive personal brand and consistent user experience.

---

# Information Architecture Goals

The information architecture should:

- Present information in a clear and logical hierarchy.
- Help visitors quickly understand who I am and what I do.
- Support multiple recruiter journeys from a single portfolio.
- Allow recruiters to explore only the specialization relevant to their hiring needs.
- Minimize unnecessary navigation.
- Maintain consistency throughout the platform.
- Scale as new projects, experiences, and skills are added over time.
- Prioritize clarity over complexity.

# Global Navigation

The primary navigation should remain minimal, elegant, and consistent across the platform.

Navigation Items

- Home
- About
- Specializations
- Selected Work
- Skills
- Education
- Certifications
- Contact

The navigation should remain identical throughout the website to create a predictable user experience.

---

# Homepage Structure

The homepage serves as the primary entry point for every visitor.

Its purpose is to introduce the personal brand, communicate professional value, and guide visitors toward the specialization most relevant to their interests.

Recommended section order:

1. Hero
2. About Me
3. What I Build
4. Explore My Specializations
5. Selected Work
6. Skills & Tools
7. Education
8. Certifications
9. Contact

The homepage should answer three questions within the first few seconds:

- Who am I?
- What can I do?
- Where should I go next?

# Specialization Architecture

Each specialization functions as a focused mini-portfolio while remaining part of one unified Personal Career Platform.

Although every specialization shares the same design language, navigation, and overall experience, the content is tailored to how recruiters evaluate candidates within each profession.

---

## UI/UX Design & Front-End Development

Purpose

Showcase user-centered thinking, interface design, and front-end development experience.

Primary Content

- Selected Projects
- Project Preview
- View Case Study
- Skills & Tools

Notes

Projects are initially presented as concise previews. Visitors may choose to open the complete case study to explore the full design process, development process, and project outcomes.

Current Planned Projects

- Attendance Management System (Academic Team Project)
- Personal Career Platform (This Portfolio)

---

## Data Analytics

Purpose

Demonstrate analytical thinking through dashboards, reports, and data-driven insights.

Primary Content

- Dashboard Projects
- Project Preview
- Project Details
- Skills & Tools

Notes

Each project focuses on the business problem, methodology, dashboard development, key insights, and recommendations.

---

## Graphic Design

Purpose

Present creative work through a curated visual gallery.

Primary Content

- Gallery
- Design Details
- Skills & Tools

Notes

Graphic Design emphasizes visual presentation rather than lengthy case studies.

Most projects include:

- Image Preview
- Project Information
- Short Description
- Optional Mockups

Large branding or identity projects may include a more comprehensive project breakdown.

---

## Executive Support & Digital Operations

Purpose

Demonstrate organizational, administrative, and operational skills that support productive teams and efficient digital workflows.

Primary Content

- Work Samples
- Work Sample Details
- Skills & Tools

Notes

Instead of traditional projects, this specialization showcases practical work examples such as:

- Productivity Systems
- SOP Documentation
- Calendar Management
- Task Management
- Research Documentation
- Meeting Notes
- File Organization
- Content Planning

Each work sample demonstrates the process, tools, and outcomes of real administrative and operational work.

# Content Taxonomy

The platform organizes work into four primary specializations.

- UI/UX Design & Front-End Development
- Data Analytics
- Graphic Design
- Executive Support & Digital Operations

Every work item belongs to one specialization while allowing multiple technology or tool tags.

Example

Specialization

UI/UX Design & Front-End Development

Tags

- React
- Tailwind CSS
- TypeScript
- Figma
- Accessibility

# Navigation Principles

Navigation should always be:

- Simple
- Predictable
- Consistent
- Responsive
- Accessible

Visitors should always understand:

- Where they are
- What they are viewing
- What actions they can take next

Navigation should never require unnecessary clicks or create confusion.

---

# Scalability

The platform should continue evolving alongside my professional career.

Future additions may include:

- Blog
- Search
- CMS Integration
- Testimonials
- Publications
- AI Assistant
- Speaking Engagements
- Additional Professional Specializations

The architecture should support these additions without requiring significant restructuring.

---

# Information Architecture Principles

Every structural decision should support the following principles:

- Organize content around user needs.
- Reduce cognitive load.
- Keep related information together.
- Guide recruiters toward relevant work quickly.
- Maintain consistency throughout the platform.
- Design for long-term scalability.
- Simplicity over complexity.
- Quality over quantity.
