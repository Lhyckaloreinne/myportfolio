# Project Summary

POSHED Portal is a mobile application designed exclusively for BS Information Technology students under the Prestigious Organization of Software and Hardware Elite Developers (POSHED). It serves as a centralized platform where members can access organization announcements, engage with fellow students, purchase official merchandise, and manage their academic tasks within a single application.

The project aimed to improve communication, strengthen student engagement, and reduce the need to switch between multiple platforms for organization-related activities.

---

# Business Context

Before the project, communication within the organization relied on various platforms such as social media, messaging applications, and manual announcements. Students also lacked a dedicated space for managing both organization activities and their personal academic responsibilities.

POSHED Portal was created to centralize these services into one mobile platform that supports communication, collaboration, and productivity.

---

# Problem Statement

Students had to use multiple applications to:

- View announcements
- Keep track of organization activities
- Purchase official merchandise
- Communicate with fellow members
- Manage academic responsibilities

As a result, important information was often scattered across different platforms, making it difficult to stay informed and organized.
