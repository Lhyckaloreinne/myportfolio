# Project Summary

Northern Cuts is an e-commerce website designed for a furniture business specializing in handcrafted wooden furniture and custom-made furniture pieces.

The website provides customers with a centralized online platform where they can browse products, explore completed work, inquire about customized furniture, and place orders without relying solely on in-person visits.

The primary objective was to establish an online presence that reflects the quality of the business while making its products and services more accessible.

---

# Business Context

Northern Cuts primarily operates as a physical furniture business.

Customers previously relied on visiting the store or directly communicating with the business to explore available products and discuss customized furniture requirements.

The project aimed to extend the business into the digital space while preserving the premium presentation of its handcrafted furniture.

---

# Problem Statement

The business lacked a centralized online platform where customers could conveniently:

- Discover available furniture
- Explore previous work
- Inquire about customized furniture
- Place orders
- Contact the business

As a result, much of the customer journey depended on physical visits or direct communication.

---

# Project Goals

## Business Goals

- Establish a professional online presence.
- Showcase handcrafted furniture online.
- Centralize product information.
- Support customer inquiries.
- Provide an online ordering experience.
- Promote customized furniture services.

## User Experience Goals

- Make furniture easy to browse.
- Keep product photography as the primary focus.
- Create a simple browsing experience.
- Reduce friction when contacting the business.
- Support both ready-made and customized furniture journeys.

# Design Considerations

Formal UX research was not conducted for this project.

Instead, design decisions were guided by:

- Client requirements
- Business goals
- Characteristics of handcrafted wooden furniture
- Customer purchasing journey
- Product presentation
- Custom furniture inquiry workflow

---

# Visual Direction

The interface was intentionally designed to complement the natural beauty of handcrafted wooden furniture rather than compete with it.

Design characteristics include:

- Neutral color palette
- Warm visual atmosphere
- Clean layouts
- Product-first presentation
- Large product imagery
- Clear navigation
- Minimal interface distractions
- Consistent visual hierarchy

# Key Design Decisions

## Neutral Visual Palette

Reason:

Allow the natural colors and textures of handcrafted furniture to remain the primary visual focus.

---

## Product-First Layout

Reason:

Furniture purchasing relies heavily on visual presentation.

Layouts were designed to emphasize photography over interface elements.

---

## Simplified Navigation

Reason:

Enable customers to browse products quickly with minimal friction.

---

## Unified Customer Journey

Reason:

Bring browsing, inquiries, and ordering into a single digital experience.

# Outcome

The project established Northern Cuts' online presence through a centralized e-commerce platform where customers can browse handcrafted furniture, submit inquiries, request customized furniture, and place orders within a unified digital experience.

---

# Reflection

This project strengthened my understanding of designing e-commerce experiences centered around physical products, where imagery, layout, and visual hierarchy directly influence customer engagement.

It reinforced the importance of allowing products to become the primary visual focus while designing intuitive pathways for browsing, purchasing, and communication.

If I were to continue developing the project, I would explore richer filtering options, wishlist functionality, enhanced product galleries, and a more interactive customization workflow.
