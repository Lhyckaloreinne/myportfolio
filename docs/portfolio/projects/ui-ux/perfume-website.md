# Project Summary
Scenty is a simple e-commerce website for a perfume and fragrance business.

The website provides customers with an online platform where they can discover fragrances, explore featured products, learn about the brand, and access different areas of the website.

The primary objective was to create a clean and straightforward digital storefront that presents perfume products clearly while maintaining a refined and visually focused brand presence.

---

# Business Context
Scenty is presented as a digital storefront for a fragrance business.

The website provides customers with an accessible online space where they can discover perfume products and learn more about the brand.

The project focuses on presenting fragrance products through a simple browsing experience while establishing a clean and recognizable online presence.

---

# Problem Statement
A fragrance business needs a simple digital platform where customers can conveniently:

- Discover available fragrances

- Explore featured products

- Learn about the brand

- Navigate the website easily

- Access contact information

The project addresses this need by providing a centralized website that combines product presentation, brand information, and contact access.

# Visual Direction
The interface was designed around a clean and minimal fragrance aesthetic, allowing the perfume products and imagery to remain visually prominent.

Design characteristics include:

- Clean white background

- Minimal color palette

- Strong black typography

- Large product imagery

- Spacious layouts

- Clear navigation

- Simple product cards

- Strong visual hierarchy

- Minimal interface distractions

# Key Design Decisions
## Minimal Visual Palette
Reason:

Keep the website clean and allow the fragrance products and imagery to remain the primary visual focus.

---

## Product-Focused Presentation
Reason:

Perfume is a highly visual product, so large and carefully presented product imagery helps users discover and understand the fragrance collection.

---

## Simple Navigation
Reason:

Keep the main website sections easy to access through a straightforward navigation structure.

---

## Featured Product Cards
Reason:

Highlight selected fragrances and provide users with essential product information such as the product name, description, price, and shopping action.

---

## Clear Brand Introduction
Reason:

Introduce the fragrance business and communicate its story while giving users additional context about the brand.

# Outcome
The project established Scenty as a simple digital storefront for a fragrance business.

The website provides a centralized experience for discovering fragrances, exploring featured products, learning about the brand, accessing contact information, and navigating the main areas of the website.

# Reflection
This project strengthened my understanding of developing a simple e-commerce website where product presentation and visual hierarchy play an important role in helping users discover products.

It reinforced the importance of keeping navigation straightforward while giving product imagery enough space to remain visually prominent.

If I were to continue developing the project, I would explore more detailed product pages, product filtering, fragrance categories, wishlist functionality, improved cart interactions, and a more complete checkout experience.
